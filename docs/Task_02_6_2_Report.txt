PHẦN 2 — TRANSITION POLICY
================================================================================

Module duy nhất chứa luật chuyển state:

  TRANSITION_POLICY.canTransition(currentState, targetState, finalDecision)

interface TransitionPolicy {
  canTransition(
    currentState: EntryState,
    targetState: EntryState,
    finalDecision: FinalDecisionResult,
  ): boolean
}

Rules:
  IDLE    → WATCH   khi có finalDecision
  WATCH   → READY   khi finalDecision != null
  WATCH   → BLOCKED khi finalDecision.halted
  READY   → ENTRY   khi decisionCount === 1 và có finalDecision
  ENTRY   → ACTIVE  luôn hợp lệ (placeholder)
  ACTIVE  → EXIT    placeholder
  EXIT    → IDLE    placeholder

Helpers (metadata graph — không evaluate policy):
  listPolicyTransitionTargets(currentState)
  getPolicyTransitionReason(from, to)
