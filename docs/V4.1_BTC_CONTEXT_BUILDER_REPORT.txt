3. LOGIC TÍNH TOÁN
================================================================================

  buildBTCContext() tái dùng calculateTrendStrength() từ trendStrengthEngine.ts
  (Engine 1 — cùng công thức V4.1_FORMULAS.md, EMA/ADX/slope từ indicators.ts).

  Bước 1 — EMA Alignment (0–40đ):
    EMA20, EMA50, EMA200 từ close
    BULL perfect  (price>EMA20>EMA50>EMA200) → 40
    BULL partial  (price>EMA20>EMA50)         → 30
    BULL weak     (price>EMA20)               → 20
    BEAR perfect  (price<EMA20<EMA50<EMA200)  → 40
    BEAR partial  (price<EMA20<EMA50)         → 30
    BEAR weak     (price<EMA20)               → 20
    NEUTRAL                                     → 0

  Bước 2 — ADX (0–35đ):
    >40  → 35
    25–40 → 25
    20–25 → 15
    <20  → 0

  Bước 3 — EMA50 Slope (0–25đ):
    |slope|>2%  → 25
    1–2%        → 15
    0.3–1%      → 8
    <0.3%       → 0

  btcTrendStrength = clamp(0, 100, sum Bước 1+2+3)

  btcDirection:
    price>EMA20 && EMA20>EMA50 → BULL
    price<EMA20 && EMA20<EMA50 → BEAR
    else                       → NEUTRAL

  btcStrengthBand (resolveBtcStrengthBand):
    ≥80     → 'strong'
    50–79   → 'moderate'
    25–49   → 'weak'
    <25     → 'none'

  btcAlignmentFactor: 0.75 (placeholder)
    Orchestrator sau này gọi resolveBTCAlignmentFactor(altDirection, …)
    với contract Engine 4 khi map sang marketConfidenceEngine.
