2. CẤU TRÚC KIẾN TRÚC V4.1
================================================================================

  Luồng scan thực tế (mỗi lần quét — tính LẠI hoàn toàn):

  Bước 0  Raw Market Data
            Binance klines 4H (symbol + BTCUSDT), limit 250, closed only
            Output: RawMarketSnapshot (OHLCV + takerBuyVolume)
                │
                ▼
  Bước 1  Market Intelligence Layer  ★ chạy TRƯỚC mọi quyết định UI
            Engine 1 → TrendStrength (0–100), trend_direction
            Engine 2 → TrendExhaustion, Volume_Divergence_pts
            Engine 3 → ReversalProbability, RSI/CVD divergence scores
            Engine 4 → MarketConfidence, BTCAlignmentFactor
            Market State Engine → 8 category (StrongUptrend … Transition)
            Output: MarketIntelligenceSnapshot
                │
        ┌───────┴───────┐
        ▼               ▼
  Bước 6          Bước 2  Visibility Manager
  Protection      INACTIVE | WATCH_MODE | TRADE_MODE | POSITION_MODE
  (song song)           │
                        ▼ (nếu WATCH/TRADE/POSITION)
  Bước 3          Opportunity Detection
                  Entry Quality ≥ 70 → tiếp
                        │
                        ▼
  Bước 4          Risk Engine (Smart SL)
                  → Profit Engine (Smart TP)
                  → Trade Setup Generator
                        │
  Open position? ───────┴──► Bước 5 Position Management
                              (đọc LẠI MI snapshot scan hiện tại)
                        │
  Bước 7          Support System (ASYNC sau ENTER/CLOSE)


  Visibility modes:
    INACTIVE       — ẩn / không trade
    WATCH_MODE     — giám sát, chưa đủ entry quality
    TRADE_MODE     — có thể mở setup mới
    POSITION_MODE  — đang có lệnh mở, ưu tiên quản lý vị thế

  Engine 1 — Trend Strength (trendStrengthEngine.ts)
    • EMA alignment BULL/BEAR/NEUTRAL
    • ADX(14) 4H, EMA50 slope
    • Output: TrendStrength 0–100, trend_direction

  Engine 2 — Trend Exhaustion (trendExhaustionEngine.ts)
    • RSI, volume vs MA20, new high/low, candle streak
    • Volume divergence (+20 pts)
    • Output: TrendExhaustion 0–100

  Engine 3 — Reversal Probability (reversalProbabilityEngine.ts)
    • findSwingPoints, getLastTwoSwings
    • calculateRSIDivergenceScore
    • calculateCVDV41, calculateCVDDivergenceScore
    • calculateReversalProbability

  Engine 4 — Market Confidence (marketConfidenceEngine.ts)
    • resolveBTCAlignmentFactor (0.5–1.0)
    • calculateMarketConfidence
    • Công thức: TrendStrength × (1 − Exhaustion/100) × BTCAlignment

  BTC Context Builder (btcContextBuilder.ts) — mới 2026-07-03
    • buildBTCContext(btcKlines4H) → BTCContext
    • resolveBtcStrengthBand (strong/moderate/weak/none)
    • Tái dùng Engine 1 scoring qua calculateTrendStrength
    • Fallback khi < 220 nến
    • Chi tiết: docs/V4.1_BTC_CONTEXT_BUILDER_REPORT.txt

  Bước 2 — Visibility Manager (visibilityManager.ts)
    • calculatePreliminaryScores (buy/sell score sơ bộ)
    • resolveVisibilityHysteresis (HIỆN/ẨN, chống nhấp nháy)
    • resolveTradeModeUpgrade (WATCH → TRADE khi entry_quality ≥ 70)
    • DEFAULT_VISIBILITY_CONFIG trong types.ts

  Types đã khai báo (types.ts) — sẵn cho layer chưa code:
    MarketState (8 enum)
    MarketIntelligenceSnapshot
    VisibilityMode, VisibilityResult, VisibilityConfig
    PositionState, OpenDirection
